# imagine booth — attendant guide (staff mode)

One page. You line a group up in front of the green screen, press **capture**, and wave the next group forward. The computer does the rest.

## 1. set up the screen (5 minutes, once a day)
1. Hang the green screen flat and light it **evenly** — no shadows, no wrinkles. Stand the group **2 big steps in front of it**.
2. Point the camera at the green, a little above eye height, and switch it on. Open imagine booth: you will see a live picture of the screen in the middle.
3. Look at the **green screen chip** on the right. It should say *green screen looks good*. If it complains, see "troubleshooting" below.
4. Optional: plug in a second screen or projector facing the queue, press **guest display**, drag that window onto it and double-click it for fullscreen. People waiting see themselves in the scene, a big countdown, and a "next group, please" banner.
5. Check the printer lamp is ready and paper is loaded. Do one test capture and print.

## 2. line people up and capture
1. Pick a **scene** along the bottom (tap it, or press **1–9**; **← →** steps through). The big picture shows exactly how the group will look in that scene.
2. Turn on **guides** if you want a centre line and a "heads here" circle to help you frame the group.
3. Press the red **capture** button (or the **space bar**, or your presenter clicker). With a countdown the screen counts down; with *instant* it shoots straight away.
4. **Burst of 3** takes three quick frames so you can pick the one where nobody blinked. Leave it off in a busy line.
5. The moment you press, the console is ready for the next group. The picture finishes in the background and appears on the left under today's list with a **ticket number** (A001, A002, ...). The same number is printed small on the bottom of the print.

## 3. what the guest receives
- A **4x6 print** with the imagine logo, the venue name, and their ticket number. If digital sharing is on for your venue the print also carries a **QR code**; scanning it opens their photo on their own phone.
- Write the ticket number on a card or say it out loud ("you're A014") so they can come back for a reprint.
- With **auto-print** on, prints start by themselves. Otherwise open the capture and press **print**.

## 4. fix, change or reprint
Tap a capture in the list (or press **[** and **]** to move through it).
- **Change scene** — tap another scene; the picture rebuilds in a second. No need to line the group up again.
- **Pick the best frame** — only after a burst: tap the frame with everyone's eyes open.
- **Name it** — type "smith family" so you can find it later.
- **Print / reprint** — set copies with + and −, then **print** (or press **P**).
- **Find a guest at the exit** — press **F** and type their number, for example `14`.
- **Retake** (or press **R**) — removes the last capture so you can shoot it again. An **undo** button appears for 8 seconds. **Delete** asks before it removes a capture.

## 5. keys
| key | does |
|---|---|
| space (or your clicker) | capture |
| 1–9, ← → | scene |
| P | print selected |
| R | retake last |
| [ ] | previous / next capture |
| F | find a ticket |
| Esc | close details / cancel countdown |
| ? | help |

## 6. troubleshooting — top 8
1. **"Green is showing on the group" or edges look fuzzy** — move the group 2 steps further from the screen and light the screen evenly. Take shadows off the screen. Ask guests not to wear green.
2. **"Can't see the green screen"** — the camera is pointing the wrong way, the lights are off, or the lens cap is on.
3. **"Almost everything is turning green-screen"** — someone is wearing green, or the camera has switched to auto white-balance. Re-check the lighting; an admin can re-sample the green in *admin → green screen*.
4. **Capture button is grey / "warming up the camera"** — the camera isn't connected. Check the USB cable, close other apps using the camera, then reload the page. A DSLR may need switching off and on.
5. **A capture says "failed · retry"** — tap it and press **retry**. If it fails twice, press **retake** and shoot again.
6. **Print failed** — check paper and ribbon and that the printer is on, then press **retry** on that capture. Prints never get lost; **reprint** works any time today.
7. **Space bar does nothing** — click on an empty part of the screen first (you may be typing in the search box), or ask an admin whether the capture key was changed in *admin → settings → operating mode*.
8. **Picture looks washed out or wrong colour** — the screen isn't evenly lit, or the wrong scene is selected. Check the scene strip, then call an admin.

*Admin:* tap **admin** at the top right and enter the PIN. Under *settings → operating mode* an admin sets staff or self-serve mode, auto-print, default copies, the ticket letter, the capture key and the countdown. **self-serve** in the top bar switches the screen to guest self-serve until an admin switches back.
